CRITIC QUEST — how to open

1. In File Explorer go to:
   course stuff\Critical self talk course\study-app

2. Double-click index.html
   It opens in your browser. No install. Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage).

If the quiz is blank, the browser blocked the local scripts. Then:
   - Right-click the study-app folder → Open in Terminal
   - Run:  python -m http.server 8765
   - Open: http://localhost:8765

What it is
- A study game over the PDFs, studies, and Joe Hudson transcripts in this course folder.
- Every question is tied to a sourced card. After you answer, you see the source and the claim.
- The books disagree on purpose (Helmstetter replace-the-tape vs Joe relate vs Gallwey quiet Self 1 vs Earley parts vs Kross distance). The quiz tests those distinctions instead of mashing them into one theory.

- Unlock the next day by mastering 8 cards on the current day (2 correct, and not sitting in the review pile).
